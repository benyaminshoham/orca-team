# Starter pack catalog

These universal packs are reusable across projects within their declared
applicability. At execution time, the runner binds the selected pack to the
concrete project documents and produces an approved project brief without
modifying the pack.

## 1. [SaaS MVP delivery](../packs/saas-mvp-delivery/manifest.md)

For a small team taking a web product from problem framing through deployable
MVP. Suggested roles: Project Manager & Orchestration Lead, Product & UX Lead,
Full-Stack Engineer, Platform/Security Engineer, and QA & Release Reviewer.

Key variants: regulated data, payments, existing design system, mobile clients,
and greenfield versus existing codebase.

## 2. [Marketing website launch](../packs/marketing-website-launch/manifest.md)

For landing sites, company sites, campaigns, and content-driven launches.
Suggested roles: Project Manager & Orchestration Lead, Brand/Content Strategist,
UX/UI Designer, Web Engineer, and Accessibility/SEO/QA Reviewer.

Key variants: CMS ownership, localization, analytics/consent, ecommerce, and
brand approval workflows.

## 3. [Data and machine-learning product](../packs/data-ml-product/manifest.md)

For analytics pipelines, predictive services, evaluations, and model-backed
features. Suggested roles: Project Manager & Orchestration Lead, Product/Domain
Lead, Data Engineer, ML Engineer/Scientist, and Responsible AI & Validation
Reviewer.

Key variants: privacy classification, online versus batch inference, labeling,
human oversight, reproducibility, and monitoring requirements.

## 4. [Mobile application release](../packs/mobile-application-release/manifest.md)

For iOS, Android, or cross-platform apps from product definition to store-ready
release. Suggested roles: Project Manager & Orchestration Lead, Product/UX
Lead, Mobile Engineer, Backend/Platform Engineer, and QA/Accessibility/Release
Reviewer.

Key variants: native versus cross-platform, offline operation, device hardware,
subscriptions, and app-store compliance.

## 5. [Research and decision report](../packs/research-decision-report/manifest.md)

For evidence reviews, market studies, technical evaluations, and executive
recommendations. Suggested roles: Project Manager & Orchestration Lead,
Research Lead, Evidence/Data Analyst, Domain Specialist, and Independent Editor
& Fact Checker.

Key variants: primary versus secondary research, confidential sources,
statistical analysis, regulated claims, and publication standards.

Each linked manifest defines the exact applicability, supported variants,
execution-time intake contract, authority boundaries, provenance, and known
limitations for that pack.

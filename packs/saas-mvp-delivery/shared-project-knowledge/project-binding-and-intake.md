# Project binding and intake

## Required source inventory

Identify the owner, version, authority, and conflicts for product briefs, research, designs, contracts, repositories, architecture records, policies, threat/risk records, data inventories, analytics definitions, operational evidence, support history, and prior tests. Never copy source documents into this pack.

## Execution-time questions

### Intent and users

- What problem is being solved, for whom, in what context, and what evidence supports it?
- What observable user and business outcomes define success or failure?
- What is the smallest testable value hypothesis, and what is explicitly not in the MVP?
- Who owns product decisions, risk acceptance, release approval, operations, and final acceptance?

### Experience and scope

- Which end-to-end journeys, user states, roles, permissions, edge cases, content, and accessibility needs must be supported?
- Which designs, brand system, components, localization, browser/device targets, and analytics definitions are authoritative?
- Which assumptions need research or prototype evidence before build commitment?

### System and delivery context

- Is this greenfield or an existing codebase? What are the stack, repository rules, environments, integrations, data stores, and known debt?
- Which interfaces, migrations, compatibility promises, vendors, deployment constraints, service objectives, and support arrangements apply?
- What schedule, budget, capacity, review cadence, release strategy, and task system are approved?

### Risk and obligations

- What data is collected, derived, shared, retained, deleted, or transferred, and how is it classified?
- Which security, privacy, legal, contractual, residency, accessibility, audit, payment, or sector requirements apply?
- What threat actors, abuse cases, availability impacts, recovery targets, and unacceptable failure modes matter?
- Which conditional variants in the manifest activate additional roles, controls, tests, or gates?

## Binding output

Create a versioned project brief outside the pack containing source inventory, confirmed facts, approved assumptions, open decisions, activated variants, role/authority map, outcome measures, scope and exclusions, artifact map, constraints, quality policy, evidence requirements, release/rollback model, and definition of done. Mark every unknown with owner and resolution condition. Obtain human approval before the PM dispatches substantive work.

## Change control

New evidence may refine implementation inside approved intent. Changes to outcomes, scope, architecture constraints, data use, risk posture, deadlines, budget, gates, or definition of done require impact analysis and the designated approval. Preserve decision history; never overwrite prior truth silently.

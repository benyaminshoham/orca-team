# Project intake and source of truth

Replace this scaffold with the reusable input contract and binding rules needed
by at least two roles. Do not copy project documents or add source citations
here; record design-source provenance in `../manifest.md`.

## Execution-time questions

- What are the concrete goal and measurable success conditions?
- Who are the users, stakeholders, decision owners, and approvers?
- What is in scope and explicitly excluded?
- Which deliverables and evidence define completion?
- Which constraints and approaches are required or forbidden?
- Which systems, tools, data, documents, and existing work are authoritative?
- Which legal, privacy, security, accessibility, and brand rules apply?
- Which conditional variants in the manifest apply to this project?

## Binding output

Require the runner to record the answers, source-document inventory, selected
variants, activated roles, assumptions, and unresolved decisions in an approved
project brief outside the read-only pack.

# Research project binding intake

Use these questions to create the runtime project brief. Record answers, sources,
selected method, assumptions, conflicts, owners, and unresolved decisions outside
this read-only pack.

## Decision and scope

- What decision, audience, research question, and measurable utility define success?
- What population/context, concepts, comparators, outcomes, jurisdictions,
  languages, dates, and exclusions bound the question?
- Which claims are descriptive, comparative, predictive, causal, normative, or advisory?

## Method and evidence

- Is this a rapid scan, narrative review, structured review, systematic review,
  primary research, data analysis, or mixed method—and why is that rigor sufficient?
- Which source types, databases, repositories, datasets, search methods,
  eligibility criteria, appraisal tools, and reporting standards apply?
- Is duplicate screening/extraction, protocol registration, statistical review,
  qualitative synthesis, or certainty grading required?

## Authority, ethics, and constraints

- Who owns the decision, method, domain judgment, data, privacy/ethics review,
  independent quality gate, publication, and final acceptance?
- What confidential data, human subjects, consent, conflicts of interest,
  copyright, attribution, embargo, or regulated-claim constraints apply?
- What timeline, budget, source-access, tooling, format, and update cadence apply?

## Evidence and completion

- Which project documents and prior work are authoritative inputs rather than evidence?
- What protocol, source log, extraction records, calculations, evidence tables,
  citations, review findings, uncertainty statement, and decision artifact define done?

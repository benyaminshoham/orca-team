# Evidence and reporting standards

## Shared rules

- Predefine the question, method, eligibility, analysis, and deviation process in
  proportion to stakes; distinguish protocol from later decisions.
- Preserve a reproducible record of queries, sources, versions, dates, screening,
  exclusions, extraction, transformations, calculations, and reviewer judgments.
- Separate project inputs, discoverable evidence, expert interpretation, and assumptions.
- Match claim strength to evidence quality, directness, consistency, precision,
  and risk of bias. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.
- Present credible alternatives, negative or conflicting results, limitations,
  uncertainty, applicability boundaries, and conflicts of interest.
- Cite the source that directly supports each material claim and verify quotations,
  numbers, dates, names, and links against the original.
- Never fabricate a citation, infer inaccessible content, or let a summary replace
  inspection of the authoritative source for consequential claims.

## Artifact conventions

Every artifact names owner, method/version, inputs, dates, inclusion/exclusion
logic, transformations, validation, limitations, and downstream use. Evidence
records use stable identifiers and preserve exact source locations where allowed.

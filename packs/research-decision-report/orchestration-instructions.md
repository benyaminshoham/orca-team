# Orchestration instructions

## Binding and approval

The PM binds project documents and intake answers into a project brief. The
Research Lead proposes the method and protocol; the Domain Specialist challenges
applicability; the reviewer checks whether planned evidence can support the
intended claims. The human approves the brief, protocol, task system, external
research actions, risk posture, and definition of done before substantive work.

## Workstreams and dependencies

1. Research Lead frames the question, definitions, source strategy, eligibility,
   appraisal, synthesis method, and deviation rules.
2. Domain Specialist validates terminology, context, consequential assumptions,
   and applicability boundaries.
3. Evidence & Data Analyst finalizes extraction and analysis schemas against the
   accepted protocol.
4. Discovery/screening and domain landscape work may run in parallel after the
   protocol gate; extraction precedes final analysis and synthesis.
5. Research Lead integrates the evidence into a draft. The independent reviewer
   audits claims, citations, logic, uncertainty, and reporting completeness.

## Ownership and handoffs

Research Lead owns protocol and synthesis. Analyst owns structured evidence and
calculation artifacts. Domain Specialist owns domain-context assessment. Reviewer
owns independent findings and publication recommendation. PM owns operational
records and acceptance routing, not substance.

- Lead to Analyst: approved protocol, source set, eligibility decisions,
  extraction schema, appraisal method, and analysis contract.
- Analyst to Lead/Domain: validated evidence table, data-quality log,
  calculations, uncertainty, and reproducibility record.
- Lead to Reviewer: stable draft, claim-evidence map, protocol/deviations,
  source inventory, analysis, and limitation register.
- Reviewer to PM/human: finding log, corrected/uncorrected status, coverage gaps,
  residual risk, and publication recommendation.

Rejected handoffs return to the accountable author. Material method changes,
new primary data collection, or claim expansion require plan approval.

## Gates, conflict, and completion

Require gates for project brief, protocol/method, source eligibility, extraction
quality, analysis verification, domain applicability, independent review, and
human closure. The Research Lead decides research method within the approved
brief; the Domain Specialist decides whether domain interpretation is credible;
the reviewer decides whether evidence and reporting meet the gate; the human
decides risk acceptance and action.

Complete only when evidence records are reproducible, material claims trace to
appropriate sources, calculations reconcile, limitations and uncertainty are
prominent, critical review findings are resolved or explicitly accepted, and the
human approves the decision artifact and closure.

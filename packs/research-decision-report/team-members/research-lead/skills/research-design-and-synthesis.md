# Research design and synthesis

## Purpose and triggers

Design and conduct a transparent evidence investigation for a decision or report.

## Required inputs

Approved brief, decision context, question, audience, constraints, source access,
applicable standards, conflicts, and required rigor.

## Workflow

1. Classify questions and claims; define concepts, scope, outcomes, and exclusions.
2. Select and justify method, protocol, search/discovery, eligibility, appraisal,
   extraction, synthesis, reporting, and deviation rules.
3. Seek relevant positive, negative, null, and conflicting evidence.
4. Apply eligibility and appraisal consistently; document judgments and changes.
5. Synthesize patterns and differences without erasing heterogeneity.
6. Map claims to evidence; state uncertainty, limits, applicability, and alternatives.

## Decision rules

- Higher-stakes or causal claims require stronger designs and independent checks.
- Do not change eligibility after seeing results without recording and justifying it.
- Prefer primary/authoritative sources; verify secondary summaries against originals.
- Do not pool or compare unlike measures without a defensible method.

## Output contract and quality

Provide protocol, source/search record, eligibility decisions, appraisal, synthesis,
claim-evidence map, deviations, limitations, and decision implications. Escalate
ethics issues, method inadequacy, critical missing evidence, or pressure to suppress uncertainty.

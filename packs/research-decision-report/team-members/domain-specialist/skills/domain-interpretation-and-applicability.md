# Domain interpretation and applicability

## Purpose and triggers

Test whether research definitions, evidence, and conclusions are meaningful and
applicable in the target domain.

## Required inputs

Approved brief, protocol, definitions, evidence tables, draft claims, target
context, stakeholder map, and known policy/practice constraints.

## Workflow

1. Normalize terminology and identify contested or context-dependent definitions.
2. Review population, setting, time, jurisdiction, mechanism, and measure fit.
3. Test assumptions against practice realities, counterexamples, and consequences.
4. Separate evidence-backed domain facts from expert inference and local preference.
5. State transfer limits, implementation dependencies, and required licensed review.

## Output contract and quality

Provide terminology decisions, context comparison, applicability matrix, domain
challenges, consequence analysis, and unresolved questions. Escalate regulated
claims, unrepresented affected groups, material expert disagreement, or unsafe inference.

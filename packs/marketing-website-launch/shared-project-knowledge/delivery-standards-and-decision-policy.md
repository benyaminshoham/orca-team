# Delivery standards and decision policy

## Evidence over assertion

Every material decision names its source, owner, rationale, affected artifacts, and validation. Marketing claims remain linked to current substantiation and required qualification. Unknowns stay visible; urgency does not convert assumptions into facts.

## Audience and task coherence

Each page has one primary audience need, one primary purpose, and a defined next step. Navigation, headings, copy, visuals, interaction, metadata, and measurement must express the same intent. Avoid dark patterns, false urgency, disguised advertising, or interfaces that make refusal materially harder than consent.

## Accessibility baseline

Default to WCAG 2.2 Level AA unless the approved brief sets a different binding target. Apply accessibility throughout content, design, implementation, and review. Include semantic structure, text alternatives, keyboard operation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, zoom/reflow, understandable labels and errors, reduced-motion behavior, accessible media, and robust names/roles/states. Automated checks supplement, never replace, manual and assistive-technology testing.

## Search integrity

Make important public content reachable through crawlable links and useful HTML content. Give pages descriptive titles, headings, metadata, canonical decisions, and index instructions appropriate to their purpose. Structured data must match visible, accurate content and its applicable feature rules. Robots controls manage crawling; they are not privacy or access controls. Search optimization must not degrade users, misrepresent content, or promise rankings.

## Privacy and data minimization

Collect only approved data necessary for named purposes. Document fields, destinations, processors, access, retention, deletion, and consent behavior. Privacy notices and UI representations must match implementation. Never expose secrets or real personal data in test artifacts. Legal/privacy owners approve jurisdiction-specific requirements.

## Release quality

Define measurable targets for critical journeys, responsive behavior, compatibility, errors, accessibility, content accuracy, search controls, and performance. Test representative templates and all high-risk or unique behavior. A defect waiver identifies impact, compensating control, owner, expiry/review point, and authorized approver.

## Decision precedence

1. Applicable law, contracts, safety/security restrictions, and authorized human decisions.
2. Approved project brief and acceptance criteria.
3. Accepted role-owned artifacts and recorded decisions.
4. This pack's default guidance.

Conflicts are surfaced to the PM; specialists explain tradeoffs but do not decide outside their authority.

# Mobile product and experience

## Purpose and triggers

Define testable mobile outcomes, journeys, and interaction behavior whenever a
project is scoped or product behavior changes.

## Required inputs

Approved brief, users, evidence, platform matrix, constraints, design system,
business rules, data/privacy posture, and technical feasibility feedback.

## Workflow

1. Frame user problem, outcome metrics, risks, and explicit exclusions.
2. Prioritize journeys by value, frequency, harm, uncertainty, and dependency.
3. Model navigation, data/state transitions, permissions, interruptions, and recovery.
4. Specify content and behavior for all material states and platform differences.
5. Define accessibility intent, analytics events, and testable acceptance criteria.
6. Review feasibility and risk with engineers and verification with the reviewer.

## Decision rules

- Prefer the smallest coherent release that proves the approved outcome.
- Follow platform conventions unless evidence supports a deliberate divergence.
- Never trade away critical accessibility, privacy, safety, or recovery behavior silently.
- Label hypotheses and assumptions; do not present them as research findings.

## Output contract

Prioritized journey map, state/interaction specification, content inventory,
analytics plan, decision log, and acceptance criteria with evidence links.

## Quality and escalation

Check complete states, traceability, accessibility, platform fit, measurable
outcomes, and feasibility. Escalate unresolved strategy, conflicting stakeholders,
missing evidence, or changes that materially alter scope or risk.
